Community Guidelines 1. Purpose

These Guidelines support a safer, constructive community across Marvel Chat's public content, Moments, text chat, Skills, Market and member-to-member interactions.

Community Guidelines 2. Respect others

Treat other members with basic dignity. Disagreement is allowed; targeted humiliation, threats, persistent abuse and demeaning attacks are not.

Community Guidelines 3. No threats or incitement

Do not threaten, encourage or coordinate violence, intimidation, or unlawful harm against a person or group.

Community Guidelines 4. No harassment

Do not repeatedly contact, target, shame or intimidate someone after they ask you to stop. Do not coordinate others to harass a member.

Community Guidelines 5. No hate-based abuse

Do not promote dehumanization, exclusion, or violence toward people based on protected or vulnerable characteristics. Criticism of ideas must not become targeted abuse of people.

Community Guidelines 6. No impersonation

Do not pretend to be another member, public figure, business, support agent or official. Do not use profile imagery or usernames to deceive others about identity.

Community Guidelines 7. Authentic accounts

Do not create accounts to evade blocks, moderation, restrictions, quotas, or to manipulate popularity, referrals or marketplace activity.

Community Guidelines 8. Privacy

Do not publish another person's private contact information, address, private messages, identity documents or location without a lawful basis and appropriate permission.

Community Guidelines 9. Account security

Never request another member's password, one-time code, private authentication link, or access token. Report messages that attempt to steal credentials.

Community Guidelines 10. Scams and deception

Do not run phishing attempts, impersonate support, make fraudulent offers, send deceptive links or misrepresent the identity or purpose of an account.

Community Guidelines 11. Spam

Do not flood the feed, comments, Moments, notifications or chats with repetitive promotions, copied content, unwanted solicitations or automated messages.

Community Guidelines 12. Manipulated engagement

Do not buy, sell, trade or automate likes, comments, follows, saves, reviews or other signals to mislead the community.

Community Guidelines 13. Respect consent in chat

Message people for relevant and respectful reasons. Do not evade a block, continue unwanted approaches, or use private chat to threaten, blackmail or pressure a member.

Community Guidelines 14. Sexual exploitation and non-consensual material

Do not share intimate material without consent, sexualize minors, exploit another person, or use the platform to seek or distribute abusive sexual content. Report such content through appropriate channels.

Community Guidelines 15. Minors and safeguarding

Do not groom, exploit, threaten or endanger minors. The company must publish and enforce a clear minimum-age and child-safety policy before public launch.

Community Guidelines 16. Dangerous conduct

Do not encourage other members to undertake dangerous challenges, illegal activity or actions likely to cause serious injury.

Community Guidelines 17. Self-harm and crisis content

Do not encourage someone to hurt themselves, glorify such harm, or use crisis-related content to threaten or manipulate others. Respond with care and report urgent safety concerns using appropriate local emergency/support channels.

Community Guidelines 18. Graphic or disturbing content

Do not post graphic material intended to shock, threaten, harass or glorify abuse. Use any supported content labels accurately and respect members who do not wish to see distressing content.

Community Guidelines 19. Illegal goods and services

Do not offer or request illegal goods, stolen property, contraband, prohibited substances, weapons or services that violate applicable law or these Guidelines.

Community Guidelines 20. Intellectual property

Share only content you own or are authorized to use, subject to lawful exceptions. Do not distribute copyrighted material, trademarks or creative work in ways that infringe others' rights.

Community Guidelines 21. Misinformation and misleading claims

Do not knowingly present fraudulent identity, qualifications, product details or transaction status. For high-impact claims, make context clear and correct material errors.

Community Guidelines 22. Respectful debate

Critique arguments and conduct rather than targeting a person's identity. Do not organize dogpiles, intimidation or deliberate disruption of a conversation.

Community Guidelines 23. Harmful links and malware

Do not distribute malware, credential-harvesting pages, malicious scripts or links intended to compromise devices or accounts.

Community Guidelines 24. No unauthorized access

Do not probe private accounts, access data without permission, bypass App Check or other safeguards, or exploit bugs for personal advantage.

Community Guidelines 25. Automation and scraping

Do not use bots or scripts to mass-contact members, scrape private content, manipulate rankings or create excessive system load without written authorization.

Community Guidelines 26. Public posts

Post relevant content and choose visibility carefully. Do not publish other people's confidential information, abusive threats or deceptive promotions.

Community Guidelines 27. Comments and reactions

Do not use comments or reactions to harass, brigading, impersonate endorsement, or manipulate a post's popularity.

Community Guidelines 28. Moments

Respect that Moments may be temporary and visible only according to current privacy rules. Do not capture, redistribute or misuse another member's Moment in ways that violate their rights or safety.

Community Guidelines 29. Respect blocks and reports

Do not retaliate against a person for blocking you or reporting potential abuse. Attempts to identify or punish a reporter may result in restrictions.

Community Guidelines 30. Accurate Skills profiles

Providers must describe services, experience, availability, location and pricing honestly. Do not claim licenses, certificates, employment or qualifications without a factual basis.

Community Guidelines 31. Skill request conduct

Requesters should communicate the requested outcome and relevant constraints respectfully. Providers should not pressure members, misrepresent availability or demand sensitive credentials.

Community Guidelines 32. Professional boundaries

Marvel Skills does not itself guarantee provider quality or qualifications. Members should use independent judgment and follow applicable professional requirements.

Community Guidelines 33. Accurate Market listings

Sellers must use truthful product descriptions, images, prices, stock and store details. Do not list counterfeit, stolen, prohibited, unsafe or unlawfully offered goods.

Community Guidelines 34. Commerce honesty

Do not falsify availability, manipulate order/request status, misrepresent shipping or fulfillment, or pretend a payment occurred when it did not.

Community Guidelines 35. No off-platform deception

Do not use listings to lure members into fraudulent transactions or to evade legitimate platform safety controls. The platform does not promise escrow, refund protection or payment guarantees unless expressly provided.

Community Guidelines 36. Reviews and feedback

Reviews and feedback must reflect genuine experiences or accurate product feedback. Do not threaten, bribe, fabricate or retaliate to manipulate reviews.

Community Guidelines 37. Reporting content

Report content you reasonably believe violates the Guidelines. Be specific and truthful, and do not use reports to silence criticism or target members unfairly.

Community Guidelines 38. Evidence and confidentiality

When reporting a safety or abuse issue, share only evidence reasonably needed for review. Do not publish reports, private correspondence or personal information to retaliate against others.

Community Guidelines 39. Moderation

Reported content may be reviewed and may be restricted while a concern is evaluated. Available enforcement depends on the risk, evidence, applicable law and the controls actually available to moderators.

Community Guidelines 40. Possible consequences

Violations may lead to content removal, feature limits, listing restrictions, suspension or account closure. Severe safety issues may require urgent measures.

Community Guidelines 41. Appeals

Where an appeal channel is published, submit a concise factual explanation and relevant information. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens or unrelated private data.

Community Guidelines 42. Respect community resources

Do not overload support, feedback or reporting tools with repetitive, knowingly false or abusive submissions.

Community Guidelines 43. Promotions and commercial transparency

Clearly identify promotional content and relevant commercial relationships where required. Do not disguise advertising as an independent recommendation, impersonate a seller or pressure members through deceptive marketing.

Community Guidelines 44. Ban evasion and coordinated abuse

Do not use alternate accounts, groups or automation to evade a valid restriction, retaliate against reporters, brigade a member, or recreate content removed for a safety reason.

Community Guidelines 45. Accessibility and inclusion

Avoid using the platform to exclude, intimidate or mock members for disability or other personal characteristics. Provide reasonable context and communicate respectfully.

Community Guidelines 46. Safety in offline interactions

Use care when arranging in-person meetings, purchases or services. Consider a public place, tell a trusted person where appropriate, and do not treat a profile as verified identification.

Community Guidelines 47. Emergencies

Marvel Chat is not an emergency-response service. If someone appears to face immediate danger, use appropriate local emergency services or trusted support; do not depend on an in-app report for urgent intervention.

Community Guidelines 48. Lawful requests

Do not obstruct a lawful safety investigation or use the service to evade legal restrictions. Requests from authorities are handled according to applicable law and the company's verified procedures.

Community Guidelines 49. Updates

These Guidelines may be revised as features and risks change. The latest published version governs platform participation subject to applicable law.

Community Guidelines 50. Contact and final review

The company must publish a monitored reporting/support contact, moderation response policy and appeal procedure before launch. Last updated 12 October 2026; this draft requires operational and legal review before public release.
