Privacy Policy 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes the personal information processed by Marvel Chat's current web application and the choices and requests available to members. Actual practices should be verified against the final deployment before this document is published.

Privacy Policy 2. Responsible organization

Marvel Chat is a product of Davonium Technologies. The legal entity name, registered address, jurisdiction and privacy contact must be confirmed by the company before launch.

Privacy Policy 3. Information you provide

The service may process the display name, username, email address, profile biography, location text, profile photo reference, posts, Moments, comments, messages, feedback, reports, listings and other information you submit.

Privacy Policy 4. Account and sign-in data

Firebase Authentication supports account creation and sign-in. Depending on your sign-in method, the service may receive a stable account identifier, email address, display name and profile image supplied by the authentication provider.

Privacy Policy 5. Username and public identity

A username and selected profile fields may be shown to other members so they can find and recognize you. Do not place confidential information in fields intended for public display.

Privacy Policy 6. Profile information

Profile data can include your name, username, biography, location text and photo reference. The interface may display a profile photo in Profile, Home, Moments and Chat where the corresponding identity information is available.

Privacy Policy 7. Posts and Moments

The service processes the text, visibility choice, media references, timestamps and related activity needed to publish and display your posts and Moments. Public content may be viewable beyond your direct contacts.

Privacy Policy 8. Comments, reactions and saves

The service processes comments, reaction state, saved-post references and associated author/content identifiers to provide these features and maintain visible counts or lists.

Privacy Policy 9. Chat messages and metadata

Chat processes message text, sender and participant identifiers, thread identifiers, timestamps, read state and supported edit/delete state. Do not assume that deleting an item in your view instantly erases every recipient copy or legally retained record.

Privacy Policy 10. Follows and connections

The service processes follower/following relationships and summary counts to display connection lists and determine which account-related content may appear in relevant views.

Privacy Policy 11. Notifications

The application processes notification records and their recipient, source, title/body, read state and timestamp to show activity updates. Some in-app actions may delete a notification from the active list.

Privacy Policy 12. Push notification tokens

If you enable browser notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging may issue a browser/device token that the application registers with the backend. You can disable notifications in supported app settings and browser permissions.

Privacy Policy 13. Browser and device identifiers

The supplied client stores random browser identifiers used for device registration in localStorage. The browser, operating system and network may also process technical request information when you access the site.

Privacy Policy 14. Preferences and local storage

The client stores selected interface preferences and limited drafts or cached peer identity data in localStorage. These values reside in the browser and may remain until cleared; clear local data on shared devices and sign out when finished.

Privacy Policy 15. Service worker and caching

The progressive web app uses a service worker to cache static application resources and an offline fallback document. This is intended to keep the app shell available, not to authorize access to another member's private content.

Privacy Policy 16. Marvel Skills data

If you use Skills, the service processes provider profile details, service listing details, categories, requests, offers and related status information required by the supported workflow.

Privacy Policy 17. Marvel Market data

If you use Market, the service processes store information, product descriptions, categories, displayed prices/currency, inventory and commerce-request information required by the current feature set.

Privacy Policy 18. Feedback and reports

Messages submitted through feedback or reporting tools may include the selected category, route, account identifier and text needed to investigate a problem or abuse report.

Privacy Policy 19. Media metadata

The media pipeline stores identifiers, owner IDs, feature, MIME type, declared/verified byte size, object key, upload state and timestamps. Media files are intended to reside in private Cloudflare R2 storage rather than in Firestore documents.

Privacy Policy 20. Signed media access

The backend may create short-lived signed URLs for authorized media upload or read operations. A person who obtains a valid signed URL may be able to use it while it remains valid; do not share a URL for private media.

Privacy Policy 21. Information from providers

Firebase/Google services process account and technical data to provide authentication and backend infrastructure. Cloudflare processes requests needed for domain delivery, Worker execution or R2 media storage as configured.

Privacy Policy 22. Use to provide the service

Information is used to authenticate members, display profiles and content, deliver messages, run the Skills and Market workflows, manage notification state and maintain settings.

Privacy Policy 23. Security and abuse prevention

Information may be used to validate requests, protect accounts, enforce visibility/ownership rules, investigate suspected misuse and maintain platform security.

Privacy Policy 24. Support and product improvement

Feedback and relevant diagnostic information may be used to respond to members and correct defects. Error and operational logs may contain technical context; secrets and authentication tokens should not be logged.

Privacy Policy 25. Legal and safety requirements

Information may be retained or disclosed where required by valid legal process, to protect a person's safety, or to address abuse and security incidents, subject to applicable law.

Privacy Policy 26. Public content and recipients

Information you choose to publish publicly may be viewed, copied or shared by other people. Information sent in chat is delivered to selected participants, who may retain or disclose their own copies.

Privacy Policy 27. No sale claim

This draft does not assert that the service sells personal information. The company's final data-sharing and monetization practices must be verified and disclosed accurately before release.

Privacy Policy 28. Third-party services

Firebase/Google and Cloudflare support parts of the infrastructure. Their processing is also governed by their own service terms and privacy documents. Any additional analytics, advertising or payment providers must be added to this Policy only if actually configured.

Privacy Policy 29. Analytics and diagnostics

The supplied frontend contains a measurement ID in configuration, but the reviewed source does not initialize a separate Firebase Analytics SDK. The team should verify the production deployment before making definitive analytics claims.

Privacy Policy 30. Data location and transfers

Infrastructure may process data in regions selected by the service and its providers, including the configured European Firestore and R2 regions. Cross-border transfer requirements must be reviewed against the company's jurisdiction and provider configuration.

Privacy Policy 31. Retention principles

Data may be retained while an account or feature is in use and for reasonable operational, security, backup or legal needs. Exact retention periods are not established by the supplied files and must be set and published by the company.

Privacy Policy 32. Deletion of posts and Moments

When supported delete actions succeed, the corresponding content record is removed or changed according to its backend contract. Media cleanup is a separate storage operation and must be verified; deleting an app record is not proof every storage object was removed.

Privacy Policy 33. Account closure and deletion requests

The supplied source does not establish a complete self-service account deletion workflow or a guaranteed completion period. The company must publish a monitored contact and verified identity procedure for account/data requests.

Privacy Policy 34. Correction and profile updates

You can edit supported profile fields in the application. Some account and transaction records may need to remain accurate for security, moderation or legal reasons even after a visible profile field changes.

Privacy Policy 35. Access requests

You may submit a request for information about your personal data through the company's published privacy contact. The company must confirm the applicable identity checks, response timelines and legal exceptions under the governing law.

Privacy Policy 36. Privacy choices

You can decide what content to publish, select supported visibility options, manage notification permission in the app/browser, and clear local browser data. The available controls depend on the current version.

Privacy Policy 37. Children and age

The service's minimum age and child-safety requirements must be set and legally reviewed before public launch. Do not use the service if you are below the minimum age that will be published for the product and your location.

Privacy Policy 38. Sensitive information

Do not submit passwords, one-time passcodes, payment-card numbers or unnecessary sensitive personal information in profile fields, public posts, messages, reports or feedback.

Privacy Policy 39. Security measures

The system uses authentication, server-side callable checks, Firestore access rules, App Check where enforced, ownership checks and private media access controls in the inspected source. No measure guarantees absolute security.

Privacy Policy 40. Security incidents

If a suspected security incident affects personal data, the organization should investigate, contain the issue, preserve needed evidence and provide notices where required by applicable law. A formal public incident-contact procedure must be finalized.

Privacy Policy 41. Cookies and similar technologies

The supplied application explicitly uses localStorage and a service worker; this document does not claim a cookie-based analytics or advertising system unless one is confirmed in the live deployment. Hosting providers may use necessary technical mechanisms under their own policies.

Privacy Policy 42. External links

Links to other websites are governed by those sites' policies. Marvel Chat cannot control the privacy practices of third parties outside its service.

Privacy Policy 43. Changes to this Policy

The date above should be updated when the Policy changes. Material changes should be communicated or consent obtained where required by law.

Privacy Policy 44. Privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing, or lodge a complaint. The final policy must describe the rights actually applicable in the company's confirmed jurisdiction.

Privacy Policy 45. Contact details

Davonium Technologies must add a verified, monitored privacy/support contact and any required legal entity details before this policy is published as final. Do not infer contact details from a domain name.

Privacy Policy 46. Governing framework

Applicable privacy law, controller/processor roles, international-transfer safeguards and regulator details require confirmation by qualified counsel. This draft deliberately does not invent a governing jurisdiction.

Privacy Policy 47. Legal basis and purpose

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for personal-data processing, the company must document the basis for each purpose, explain it clearly and avoid reusing information for incompatible purposes without an appropriate basis.

Privacy Policy 48. Data controller and processors

The final policy must identify the responsible legal entity and accurately distinguish its role from service providers that process information on its behalf. Do not assume a provider's role without checking the deployed configuration and applicable contracts.

Privacy Policy 49. Automated processing

The inspected source does not establish a material automated decision-making or profiling system. If such processing is added, the company must explain its purpose, effects and applicable choices or rights before launch.
